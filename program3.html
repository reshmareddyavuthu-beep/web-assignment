🥗 Nutritious Recipes

Fresh Vegetable Salad

Vegetarian

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup chopped cucumber
  • 1 tomato
  • 1/2 cup grated carrot
  • Lemon juice and a pinch of salt

Preparation: Mix all ingredients in a bowl and serve fresh.

Calories: Approximately 100 kcal per serving

Healthy Oatmeal

Breakfast

Ingredients:

  • 1/2 cup oats
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 small banana
  • A few nuts

Preparation: Cook oats with milk and add sliced banana and nuts.

Calories: Approximately 300 kcal per serving

Vegetable Lentil Soup

Lunch / Dinner

Ingredients:

  • 1/2 cup cooked lentils
  • 1/2 cup chopped vegetables
  • 2 cups water
  • Spices and salt

Preparation: Cook the lentils and vegetables with water and spices until tender.

Calories: Approximately 220 kcal per serving

💚 Healthy Eating Tips

  • Eat a variety of fruits and vegetables.
  • Include protein-rich foods in your meals.
  • Choose whole grains such as oats and brown rice.
  • Drink water regularly throughout the day.
  • Eat regular, balanced meals.
  • Enjoy your favourite foods as part of a balanced diet.
